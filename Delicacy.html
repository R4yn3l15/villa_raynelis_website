Delicacy

A Restaurant Cleanliness Rating Website

Our project is designed to bring official NYC restaurant inspection data and authentic user reviews together to provide a more reliable and transparent restaurant hygiene system.


Problem

Datasets are very ugly and hard to look at. Most people do not know where to find public data, and thus it can be hard to verify that restaurants are accurately reporting their inspection results. Most food applications are based on food reviews rather than hygiene.


Solution

Our web application, Delicacy, will fix this. It will display a given restaurant's historic letter grade and violation information. Users will be able to leave comments on the establishment indicating real-time hygiene conditions, with the option to add photos as well. By using the open-data and user reviews, Delicacy will put food safety into the hands of the community for more accurate, frequent, and better reporting to keep those communities safe.
